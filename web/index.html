Words and look (optional)

Every word on the film, and its colours. In any line, {N} is how many notes there are, {SUNG} and {WHISTLED} how many of each, {TITLE} and {CREDIT} the title and credit above, and {PART} (in the solo line) the instrument heard alone. An empty line is left off the film.
